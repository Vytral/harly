description: "Set up your workspace, invite teammates, choose your language, and test the first job and application."

After you claim the owner account, set up the workspace and submit a test
application to check what your team and candidates will see.

## Before you begin

- Complete [quickstart](/quickstart) and claim the owner account.
- Have your recruiting team, departments, regions, and approval rules ready.
- Decide whether candidates should use the portal and which public domain will
  host your career page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workspace profile">
    Open **Settings → Workspace** and set the organization name, logo,
    time zone, and public origin. Use the same origin configured in `HARLY_URL`
    for links sent in email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the recruiting team">
    Open **Settings → Members** and invite each teammate with the narrowest
    role they need. For example, assign an Interviewer to the jobs they will
    interview for. See [built-in roles](/security/access-control) before
    granting workspace-wide access.

    <Warning>
      Do not share an owner account. Every action is attributed to a member so
      audit records and access reviews remain useful.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create pipeline stages">
    Open **Settings → Pipelines**, create the stages that match your process,
    and keep the order intentional. A practical starting point is `Applied`,
    `Screen`, `Interview`, `Offer`, and `Hired`, with a separate rejected or
    withdrawn outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a job">
    Enter a title to save a draft. Before publishing, add a description or at
    least one job section, then review the pipeline and application questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the candidate experience">
    Open the public career page in a private browser window. Submit a test
    application and verify that it appears in the expected stage. If you
    configured outbound email, check that the receipt reaches your test mailbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your language

Harly uses your browser's language on your first visit. The app supports
English, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and Dutch. Choose a
different language under **Settings → Account** or from the language menu
on the sign-in and onboarding screens. Each teammate can choose their own.

In **Account settings**, use **Email language** to choose the language of
system emails Harly sends you. Candidates have their own **Email language**
setting in their [portal profile](/product/candidate-portal). Harly uses it
for application, interview, and offer emails. The default for system emails
is English.

Harly localizes built-in templates, notifications, and default pipeline names.
Review any text your team writes before publishing jobs or sending messages
in another language.

## Your first hour

After setup, the dashboard shows **Your first hour**. It walks through inviting a
teammate, creating and publishing a job, getting candidates into the pipeline,
connecting email and a calendar, and starting an automation. Each step is checked
against your real records, so it only shows as done once the workspace actually
has the data.

To try the pipeline before real candidates arrive, choose **Load sample data** in
the candidates step. Harly adds one draft job titled "Sample · Senior Product
Engineer", eight candidates across the stages, a few notes and one interview. Sample
candidates use reserved `.invalid` email addresses, so no message can reach them. The
sample job is a draft, so it never appears on your careers page. While sample data
exists, the dashboard shows a banner.

**Remove sample data** deletes the records the sample created and leaves everything
you have since done with them. A sample record is kept, and becomes an ordinary
record, when you have turned it into real data: a sample candidate or job that an
offer, interview, document request, tag, note or hiring-team member still refers to,
a candidate whose email you changed to a real address, a job you published, or an
application you moved onto a real job. A kept candidate that still has a sample
address gets a retained `.invalid` address, so you can load the sample again.

Owners and admins can hide the checklist from its footer. **Show setup checklist** in
the account menu brings it back.
