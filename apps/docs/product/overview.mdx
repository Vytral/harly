A job has its own pipeline. Each time someone applies, Harly creates an
application for that job and links it to the person's candidate profile.
Open the application to see where they are in the process and review their
interviews, scorecards, and offer.

## How data is organized

| Record | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace | Members, jobs, candidates, settings, and integration connections. |
| Job | Pipeline stages, the hiring team, application questions, and the public listing if published. |
| Candidate | The person's profile, notes, tags, and files across their applications. |
| Application | One candidate's progress on one job, including their stage, status, and hiring history. |
| Interview | Time, attendees, location or video link, and any synced calendar event. |
| Scorecard | An interviewer's ratings and notes on an application. |
| Offer | Terms, status, and any signature collected through Harly Sign or DocuSeal. |
| Talent pool | A collection of people to consider for future roles. |

[Access control](/security/access-control) explains which records teammates can
see and how to limit a role to assigned jobs, departments, or regions.

## What the candidate sees

The [career page](/product/career-page) lists public jobs and lets people apply.
In the [candidate portal](/product/candidate-portal), candidates sign in to
follow their own applications, respond to offers, and upload requested files.
Internal notes and scorecards stay in the workspace.
