Documents keep the files a hire produces on the candidate record instead of
scattered across inboxes. These include resumes, offer letters, signed
agreements, right-to-work proof, and certifications. You can generate a
document from a template, upload a file, request one from a candidate, and send
any PDF for signature.

## Documents hub

Open **Documents** to see every file in your workspace. The **Files** tab lists
documents. The **Templates** tab lists reusable document templates if you can
manage templates.

- **Status filters:** switch between **Active**, **Awaiting signature**,
  **Expiring in 30 days**, **Date passed**, and **Archived**. **Date passed**
  only shows documents whose date has lapsed and that are not signed.
- **Filters:** narrow the list by owner, category, signature status, or the
  candidate or job a document is linked to.
- **Sorting:** sort by **Recently updated**, **Oldest update**, **Name A to Z**,
  **Name Z to A**, or **Expires soonest**.
- **Drop to upload:** drag files anywhere onto the page to upload them. Harly
  accepts PDF, DOCX, and image files up to 25 MB. If you filtered the view by a
  linked candidate or job, dropped files are linked to that record too.
- **Bulk actions:** select documents to set a category, archive, restore, or
  delete them together.

## Generate a document from a template

Templates are reusable documents with candidate details filled in, such as an
offer letter or an agreement. Generate one by hand for a candidate, or let an
[automation](/developers/automations) create it. Generated PDFs land in
your document library.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    On the candidate profile, open the **Documents** section and choose
    **Generate document**. You can also choose **Generate from template** in the
    documents hub, or start from a template on the **Templates** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template and application">
    Select a template, then the candidate application the document is for. From
    the hub, search by candidate or job name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    Harly renders the filled-in text. If the candidate is missing a detail the
    template uses, Harly warns you that the field will be left blank in the PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Choose **Generate PDF**. Harly saves the PDF to the library, linked to the
    candidate and application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Request a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    From the candidate record, choose **Request document** and name what you
    need. For example, "Proof of eligibility to work."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    The candidate receives the request in their [portal](/product/candidate-portal)
    and uploads the file there. No email attachments to chase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Uploaded documents appear on the candidate record marked as pending review.
    Accept or ask for a replacement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Document page

Each document has its own page with a preview, details, people, an activity
timeline, and a signature card. The signature card shows one primary action
based on the document's state. For example, **Send for signature** on an
unsigned PDF, or **Signed PDF** on a completed one.

### Versions

The versions panel lists every version with its number, upload date, uploader,
size, and checksum. The current version is marked **Current**. Download any
version with its download button. Older versions stay available for audit.

Choose **New version** to upload a replacement file. Signed documents are final.
To change a signed document, upload the revised file as a new document.

## Send a document for signature

Native signing requires **Remote signing links** to be enabled in
**Settings → Signature**. See [signatures](/integrations/signatures) for
signing settings and DocuSeal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the prepare dialog">
    On the document page, choose **Send for signature**. Harly opens
    **Prepare for signing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add signers">
    Enter each signer's name and email. Choose **Add signer** to add more.
    People sign in the order listed. Each signer receives their link only after
    the previous signer finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place fields">
    Pick a signer, then add **Signature**, **Date**, or **Text** fields and drag
    them into place on the PDF. Each signer needs at least one signature field.
    Date fields default to the day the signer signs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a message and send">
    Optionally add an email subject and a message for the signers, then choose
    **Send signing link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PDFs with rotated pages cannot be sent. Re-export the PDF without rotation
  first.
</Note>

You can also choose **Sign myself** to sign a PDF directly in the dashboard, or
**Record offline signature** to note a signature collected on paper.

### Track signers

The signature card lists each signer in order with their status. Depending on
the status, it shows when they were invited, last reminded, signed, or
declined, plus the decline reason and when their link expires.

For a signer who has not signed yet, you can:

- **Remind:** email a fresh link. Earlier links keep working. Manual reminders
  are limited to one per hour per signer and do not count toward the automatic
  reminder limit.
- **Copy link:** copy a new signing link to share yourself. The new link
  replaces the one in their inbox.

When every signer finishes, download the **Signed PDF** and the
**Certificate**. The certificate lists each signer, when they signed, how they
were verified, and the document fingerprint. Harly also emails the signed copy
and certificate to the sender and every signer.

### Reminders

Harly sends automatic reminders to the signer whose turn it is. The interval is
measured from when the current signer was invited or last reminded, not from
when the document was first sent. By default, Harly reminds every 3 days, at
most 3 times per signer. Change both values in
[signature settings](/integrations/signatures#reminders-and-link-lifetime).

Each reminder includes a fresh link. Links from earlier emails keep working
until the signer finishes.

### Void a request

Choose **Void request** to cancel a signature request in progress, and enter a
reason. If someone has already signed, Harly warns you before you confirm.
Signatures already given stay in the audit record, and Harly keeps the partially
signed copy as evidence. They are not carried over, so a new request starts from
scratch.

### Signer experience

Signers open the secure link on any device, including a phone. No account is
required.

1. **Verify email:** if email OTP is enabled, the signer requests a code and
   enters it. They can choose **Resend code** if it does not arrive.
2. **Fill fields:** the signer draws or types a signature once, and it fills
   every signature box assigned to them. They complete any required text
   fields. Date fields are filled with today's date.
3. **Consent:** the signer accepts the e-sign disclosure. Harly stamps the
   consent time on the server and stores it with the disclosure version.
4. **Sign:** the signer chooses **Sign document** and can download their copy.
   Harly also emails a signed-copy receipt with the document attached.

A signer can choose **Decline to sign** and optionally give a reason. Declining
ends the whole request. Harly notifies the sender with the reason and shows it
on the signer's row. You can edit the document and send it again.

## Retention and expiry

Documents carry personal data, so Harly supports retention and expiry:

- **Expiry:** a document (for example a certification) can carry an expiry date
  and surface when it lapses.
- **Retention:** an opt-in retention job anonymizes or removes documents after
  a defined period, in line with your [privacy settings](/security/privacy).
- **Legal hold:** a document under legal hold is protected from deletion until
  the hold is released.

<Warning>
  Documents are personal data. Do not download candidate documents to personal
  devices or accounts. Deletion and export must follow the
  [privacy workflow](/security/privacy).
</Warning>
