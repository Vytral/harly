---
title: "Privacy, retention, and AI operations"
description: "Operate candidate data responsibly with retention, privacy notices, access reviews, and human oversight."
---

Harly provides controls for candidate data, but the organisation operating a
workspace remains responsible for its legal assessment, notices, contracts,
and regulatory decisions. This page is an operational checklist, not legal
advice.

## Before collecting candidate data

- Publish the workspace privacy policy, candidate privacy notice, cookie policy,
  terms, and AI transparency notice.
- Record a lawful basis and retention period for each purpose, including talent
  pools and optional AI features.
- Configure candidate and talent-pool retention settings.
- Keep a current processor and subprocessor register.
- Assign an owner for data-subject requests and deletion exceptions.

## AI review

For each AI feature, document its purpose, provider, model, input data, output,
users, risks, and human reviewer. Candidate scoring is advisory and must not
be the sole basis for a hiring decision.

<Info>
  Do not place provider credentials, private notes, or unnecessary candidate
  data into prompts. Review the AI usage log and keep a documented override
  path for every material recommendation.
</Info>

## Data-subject requests

The candidate portal provides export and deletion-request controls. Verify the
requester's identity, assess legal holds and retention duties, record the due
date and outcome in a separate request register, and communicate the result.

## Incident response

1. Contain the incident and preserve relevant evidence.
2. Identify affected systems, data, people, recipients, and time window.
3. Record the assessment and corrective actions.
4. Follow the notification obligations that apply to your organisation and
   jurisdiction.
5. Revisit access, notices, processor contracts, and the DPIA if the change is
   material.
