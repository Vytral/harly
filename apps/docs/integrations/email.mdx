---
title: "Email, inbound replies, and mailboxes"
description: "Configure outbound email, reply routing, SMTP, Resend, IMAP, and provider webhooks."
---

Harly can send candidate email through Resend or SMTP and can attach replies to
the correct application through an inbound provider or mailbox connection.

## Outbound email

Open **Settings → Email**, choose a provider, configure the sender identity,
and send a test message to a controlled mailbox.

### Resend

Use a verified domain and an API key with the minimum permissions required by
your provider policy. A deployment-level `RESEND_API_KEY` can act as a default,
but workspace configuration is preferred for multi-tenant operation.

### SMTP

Configure the SMTP host, port, TLS mode, username, password, and sender. Use
port `465` for implicit TLS or `587` for STARTTLS according to your provider.
Never test with a real candidate until the sender domain passes authentication.

## Inbound replies

Configure a receiving hostname such as `replies.example.com` in **Settings →
Email**. Enter only the hostname—not a URL, port, or localhost address.

The provider webhook points to Harly's inbound-email endpoint and includes the
workspace webhook secret. Harly generates a unique `reply+token@domain`
address for each application.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure the receiving domain">
    Add the provider's MX or inbound-routing records and verify the domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Harly inbound endpoint">
    Use the endpoint shown in the workspace email settings and keep its secret
    in the provider's secret field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a controlled reply">
    Reply to a test candidate message, then confirm the message appears in the
    application thread with the correct sender and attachments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- No outbound email: check the sender domain, provider credentials, and the
  email outbox status in the operations view.
- Reply not attached: verify the reply domain is a hostname and that the
  provider preserved the `Reply-To` address.
- Duplicate delivery: email is at-least-once; use provider message IDs and the
  Harly delivery log to identify retries.
- Invalid old domain: Harly omits the Reply-To address rather than rejecting an
  entire message. Correct the workspace setting and resend.
