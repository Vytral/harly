# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Harly to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful of differing opinions and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use Harly and for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as an address or email,
  without their explicit permission;
- sharing candidate or other personal data from a real hiring process;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and any channel run by the project) and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately through the
[GitHub profile of the maintainer](https://github.com/Molret) or by
[opening a private security advisory](https://github.com/Vytral/harly/security/advisories/new)
titled "Conduct report". Reports are handled promptly, fairly and as
confidentially as possible.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban a participant, depending on the severity and whether it is
repeated.
