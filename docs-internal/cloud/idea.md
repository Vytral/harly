# OpenHire Cloud — Idea

## What is this?

The managed version of OpenHire. Same codebase as the core, deployed and operated by
the OpenHire team at app.openhire.dev. Users sign up, create a workspace, and get a
working ATS without touching a server.

## Who is it for?

Teams who want the OpenHire experience but don't want to manage infrastructure.
They might be: non-technical founders, small agencies, companies without a DevOps person,
or developers who just don't want the hassle.

## Problem

Self-hosting is great for developers. It is terrible for everyone else. The cloud offering
closes this gap — same product, no ops burden, generous free tier.

## Differentiation

- No feature disparity vs. self-hosted (unlike some open-source SaaS)
- Generous free tier: 1 workspace, 3 active jobs, unlimited candidates
- Simple, honest pricing for paid tiers
- Data export always available — no lock-in even on cloud

## Non-goals

- Not trying to become an enterprise SaaS with 50-page contracts
- Not adding cloud-exclusive features that fragment the product
- Not charging for things that are free in self-hosted without clear justification

## Success criteria

- Free tier usable as a real ATS for a small team
- Paid conversion rate > 5% of active free workspaces
- Churn < 10% monthly on paid plans
- NPS > 50

## Monetization model (draft)

| Plan | Price | Limits |
